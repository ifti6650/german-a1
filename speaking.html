Speaking practice

Sprechen

Have a voice conversation with an AI tutor, play the study-guide dialogues, check your pronunciation and answer questions out loud.

Speech recognition is not available in this browser. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone to speak; you can still listen and type your answers here.

Talk with an AI tutor

Pick a lesson and a practice type. Copy the prompt, open ChatGPT or Gemini, paste it, then switch on voice mode and start talking. Both apps have free plans with voice (with daily limits).

Open ChatGPT ↗ Open Gemini ↗
  1. On your phone (best): open the ChatGPT or Gemini app, paste the prompt and send it, then tap the voice button (ChatGPT: the sound-wave icon; Gemini: “Live”).
  2. On a computer: paste the prompt at chatgpt.com, send it, then click the voice icon next to the message box.
  3. Speak slowly. Say “Hilfe” when you are stuck, “Noch einmal, bitte” to hear it again.
  4. At the end, ask for feedback: “Bitte gib mir Feedback.”

Dialogue role-play

The site reads the other role out loud. When it is your turn, press the microphone and say your line. You pass a line with 80% of the words right.

Pronunciation trainer

Listen to each word, then say it. The browser checks whether it understood you. Nouns: say them with the article.

Answer questions about yourself

The question is read out loud. Answer in a full sentence. Your answer is written down so you can check it, or send it to the AI tutor for corrections.
