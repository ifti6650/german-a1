How these numbers work
  • Lesson progress = done sections ÷ available sections. Learn is done when you press “I’ve read this”; Wortschatz is done when ≥ 80% of the lesson's words are Known in the trainer; Practice and Apply when you finish a round; Quiz when you pass it (≥ 60%).
  • Lesson completed = lesson quiz passed. Completion is not the same as mastery.
  • Accuracy = correct answers ÷ all graded answers you gave (practice, tests, reviews, Wortschatz). Self-checked writing and speaking are not counted.
  • Mastered mistakes = mistakes answered right in 3 separate sessions, at least 12 hours apart.
  • Vocabulary retention = words marked Known in the Wortschatz trainer (its own spaced repetition).
  • Streak = consecutive days with at least one saved answer.
