Features

Everything you need to pass A1

Each tool does one job well, and they all feed one record of your progress.

Structured lessons

Every lesson has clear objectives, a Learn section with tables and common mistakes, practice, an applied task and a quiz.

Wortschatz trainer

All 783 words with article, plural and example. List, flashcards, writing and quiz, with spaced repetition that brings words back when they are due.

12 exercise types

Multiple choice, gap fill, word order, matching, articles, conjugation, translation, listening, dictation, reading, writing and speaking.

Mistake trainer

Every wrong answer is saved with its explanation and comes back until you answer it correctly in three separate sessions.

Tests with a timer

A quiz for every lesson, a test for every module and an assessment for A1.1 and A1.2, with a breakdown by skill and topic.

Your personal dashboard

See where you stopped, your accuracy, what is due for review and which topics to practise next, on every device.

How it works

A simple routine for every lesson

  1. Learn

    Read the short explanation with examples and the typical mistakes to avoid.

  2. Practise

    Train the lesson’s words and grammar with instant feedback after every answer.

  3. Test

    Pass the lesson quiz (60 %) to complete the lesson, then take the module test.

  4. Review

    Your mistakes and due words come back at the right time until they stick.
