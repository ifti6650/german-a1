# Deutsch im echten Leben

## German A1.1 + A1.2 · Real-Life Communication Guide

**24 lessons · 192 everyday situations · German-first learning**

This practical communication guide helps you **use German in real life**, not just study rules. It contains independently written dialogues, response alternatives, brief English chapter introductions, short word meanings, sentence-pattern reminders and speaking challenges.

**How to use it:** Read → Speak both roles → Change the details → Respond without reading → Repeat a few days later.

The separate **Grammar Book** explains grammar in depth. The separate **Practice & Exercises** area handles quizzes and scoring. This guide focuses on confident real-life communication.

### Learning routine

- **Day 1:** Read four situations and speak both roles.
- **Day 2:** Read the next four, then change the details in two dialogues.
- **Day 3:** Choose two situations and respond without looking.
- **Day 7:** Revisit the hardest situation and speak more freely.
- **Day 14:** Repeat one full conversation to check retention.

**Note:** All conversations and examples in this document are newly authored for this guide. The content is designed around general CEFR A1 communication goals, not official publisher materials or examinations.
